# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers and Technical Leads provide technical direction, improve delivery predictability, and help the team turn product goals into sustainable implementation plans.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical approach, architecture, and engineering standards
- Help scope and estimate work realistically with product and project leads
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and staffing constraints
- Support delivery health through planning, prioritization, and review practices
- Partner with developers to unblock implementation and maintain quality

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: align delivery feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs against business outcomes
- With Project Managers: coordinate milestones, dependencies, risk mitigation, and schedule realism
- With Developers: provide technical mentoring, architecture guidance, and implementation accountability
- With QA/Testing: align test strategy, release readiness, and defect prioritization
- With Stakeholders: translate technical constraints and status into clear, actionable communication

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable solutions on schedule
- Reduce technical and delivery risk
- Keep engineering capacity and quality aligned with project goals

---

## UX/UI Designer / User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers ensure that solutions are usable, valuable, and aligned with customer needs before and during delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, usability testing, and design validation
- Translate customer needs into user flows, requirements, and UX acceptance criteria
- Collaborate on prototypes and design decisions with product and technical teams
- Ensure accessibility, consistency, and usability in product experiences

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: validate customer problems, desired outcomes, and feature prioritization
- With Project Managers: provide design milestones, review needs, and dependency planning
- With Developers: define interaction patterns, responsive requirements, and design implementation details
- With QA/Testing: validate critical user journeys and acceptance criteria for usability
- With Stakeholders: surface customer insight and evidence to support decisions

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce rework by validating assumptions early
- Keep product decisions grounded in user evidence

---

## Business Analyst / Product Operations

### Role Summary
Business Analysts and Product Operations partners connect strategic goals, customer needs, and operational realities into clear, actionable requirements and process clarity.

### Responsibilities
- Translate stakeholder needs into structured requirements and backlog inputs
- Clarify workflows, dependencies, and decision points across functions
- Support traceability from goals to milestones, requirements, and acceptance criteria
- Help maintain documentation, operational readiness, and cross-functional coordination

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: refine problem statements, prioritization inputs, and requirement quality
- With Project Managers: coordinate project clarity, dependencies, and communication needs
- With Developers: ensure requirements are well-defined and testable
- With QA/Testing: align acceptance criteria and validation scenarios
- With Stakeholders: collect input and provide consistent status and process clarity

### Goals
- Improve requirement quality and decision readiness
- Reduce ambiguity and handoff friction between teams
- Support better planning and operational alignment

---

## Release / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Engineers help teams deliver safely, reliably, and consistently by preparing environments, deployment processes, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Own deployment pipelines, environment readiness, and release coordination
- Support monitoring, observability, rollback planning, and post-deployment verification
- Help teams maintain service health and production readiness
- Coordinate with cross-functional partners during launch windows and incidents

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: align release timing, customer impact, and communication needs
- With Project Managers: coordinate deployment windows, readiness checks, and risk mitigation
- With Developers: validate deployment readiness, automation, and rollback considerations
- With QA/Testing: verify smoke tests, release validation, and production readiness
- With Stakeholders: communicate product readiness and operational risks

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and downtime
- Improve deployment reliability and recovery speed
- Support confidence in release quality and production stability

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring real-world usage and service insight into planning, acceptance, and post-release improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer issues, operational pain points, and support needs
- Help validate readiness from an operational and service perspective
- Support stakeholder communication before, during, and after releases
- Offer user feedback to inform backlog refining and improvement work

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: provide customer-impact data and prioritization context
- With Project Managers: flag service readiness concerns and communication dependencies
- With Developers: share operational issues, incident patterns, and support trends
- With QA/Testing: contribute to acceptance scenarios and real-world validation conditions
- With Stakeholders: provide practical service insight and post-release feedback

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and issue resolution speed
- Ensure operational reality informs product decisions
- Strengthen handoffs between delivery and customer-facing teams

---

## Security / Privacy / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security, Privacy, and Compliance partners ensure work meets required controls, reduces risk, and remains aligned with policy and regulatory obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, accessibility, and compliance requirements early
- Review controls, risk treatment plans, and approval gates
- Support secure design and implementation decisions across the project lifecycle
- Help assess and communicate risk to product and leadership stakeholders

### How they interact with existing roles
- With Product Managers: define non-functional requirements and risk trade-offs
- With Project Managers: integrate controls, review gates, and escalation paths into project plans
- With Developers: advise on secure implementation practices and evidence of compliance
- With QA/Testing: validate security and privacy requirements in test coverage
- With Stakeholders: communicate critical risks, controls, and mitigation progress

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure and compliance gaps
- Maintain trust through secure and responsible delivery
- Keep delivery aligned with policy and regulatory obligations

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles do not replace the core project team; they clarify where specialized accountability and collaboration are needed for successful delivery.
